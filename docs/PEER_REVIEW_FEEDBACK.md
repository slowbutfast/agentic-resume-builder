# Peer Review Guidelines & Content Heuristics

This document synthesizes peer review feedback (Samarth, Pranav, Winston) and developer heuristics for writing high-impact software engineering resumes.

---

## 🎯 High Priority: Bullet Point Structure & Wording

### 1. Plain-English "First Bullet Rule"
- **Guideline**: The very first bullet point (`Bullet #1`) under any project or experience entry MUST clearly state what you built and what it does in plain, accessible terms without burying the recruiter in dense jargon.
- **Structure**:
  - **Bullet #1**: System purpose, core functionality, and high-level goal.
  - **Bullets #2 & #3**: Granular technical mechanisms, algorithms, database architectures, and performance metrics.

### 2. Research & Academic Roles Balance
- **Guideline**: Avoid making all bullets overly academic or jargon-heavy.
- **Target Balance**:
  - 1 bullet explaining the research/business problem and user impact.
  - 1–2 bullets detailing the engineering pipeline, stack, and caching/performance optimizations.

### 3. Edge-to-Edge Fill & Widow Prevention
- **Guideline**: Multi-line bullets should span **85%–95% of printable line width**.
- **Widow Rule**: Never leave fewer than 4 trailing words on the final line of a bullet. Either trim 2–3 words or expand by 10–15 characters to fill the line out.

## 🎨 Medium Priority: Formatting & Visual Aesthetics

### 1. Selective Bolding Strategy
Apply bolding strategically to create visual anchors for recruiters skim-reading the resume:
- **Bold Quantitative Metrics**: e.g., **97% reduction**, **5+ hours weekly**, **12,000+ ops/sec**, **sub-50ms**.
- **Bold Primary Stack Names**: e.g., **React 19**, **PostgreSQL**, **FastMCP**, **OpenAI Responses API**.
- **Avoid Over-Bolding**: Do NOT bold entire sentences or long phrases, which creates visual clutter.

### 2. Standardized Date Formatting
- Ensure date strings follow identical formatting across both Experience and Projects:
  - Format: `MMM YYYY – Present` or `MMM YYYY – MMM YYYY` (e.g., `Jun 2026 – Present`, `Aug 2025 – May 2026`).

---

## 📐 Content Strategy: Section Prioritization

1. **Relevant Coursework**: Keep to 1 max line listing 4–5 core CS subjects (e.g., *Data Structures & Algorithms, Intro to Software Engineering, Computer Systems, Discrete Probability*).
2. **Interests Section**: Keep as a single line at the very bottom as a behavioral interview icebreaker. If extra vertical room is needed for engineering projects, cut or condense.
